# Voice & Mix

Voice & Mix converts reviewed text tracks into aligned speech audio and rendered media outputs.

## Language

**Narration Track**:
Synthesized speech audio generated from a sustained audio-description track.
_Avoid_: Audio-description track, voice-over notes

**Dubbing Track**:
Target-language speech audio generated from a reviewed translation track and aligned to the source video.
_Avoid_: Translation track, narration track

**Mixdown**:
A rendered media output that combines the source video with selected narration or dubbing tracks.
_Avoid_: Export, preview
